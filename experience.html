The work

  • Extended SEMLOPs — the internal MLOps platform, nicknamed PADO — and built & integrated the internal pado_mlops Python package to streamline pipeline deployment and management.
  • Managed Kubernetes clusters and dedicated image nodes across dev and prod, and owned the container images and repositories — JFrog in dev, Harbor in prod — including cleanup and hygiene.
  • Monitored platform performance with Prometheus and Grafana dashboards, Kibana dashboards (KQL), and Cocktail Cloud memory data in production.

  • Built Python demos (GAN, regression, classification) on sample datasets to showcase the platform.

Explorations

  • Learned RAG while working on AI projects at the company, and explored LLM + RAG for a Kubernetes log parser — log collection surfaced in Kibana.
  • Ran image quality and generation POCs — SSIM, PSNR, cosine similarity, FAISS indexing, FID/KID — plus a GAN (SiT) diversity evaluation on the MLCC dataset and GAN-based drift detection.

Outside the work

  • Took Korean lessons about four evenings a week after office hours — and earned the Best Korean Student award, the only time it was ever given.
  • PADO won 1st prize at Tech Forum 2025 — every member of the project in the frame.
  • Weekend treks to Nandi Hills and Ramadevarabetta, team dinners, and a pile of Samsung goodies.
